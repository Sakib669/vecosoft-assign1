# Figma Prototype Submission Guide

This directory contains production-ready, high-fidelity vector UI assets designed specifically for your Figma submission.

All 3 mandatory situational states are represented:
1. `01_delayed_order_390x844.svg` — Screen 1: Delayed in Transit (Weather hold, revised ETA, $15 courtesy credit)
2. `02_delivered_not_received_390x844.svg` — Screen 2: Delivered but Not Received (Dispute banner, proof photo check, claim filing)
3. `03_tracking_not_available_390x844.svg` — Screen 3: Tracking Not Available Yet (Warehouse fulfillment pipeline, dispatch timer, SMS alert toggle)

---

## ⚡ How to Create Your Figma Link in 60 Seconds:

1. **Open Figma**:
   - Go to [https://figma.com/new](https://figma.com/new) in your web browser.

2. **Import the Screens**:
   - Open your project folder: `assigment-demo/figma-assets/`
   - Select and drag all 3 `.svg` files directly onto the Figma canvas:
     - `01_delayed_order_390x844.svg`
     - `02_delivered_not_received_390x844.svg`
     - `03_tracking_not_available_390x844.svg`
   - *Figma will immediately convert them into native, scalable vector frames and layers.*

3. **Arrange & Name Frames**:
   - Position the 3 mobile artboards side-by-side with ~40px spacing.
   - Name each frame:
     - `State 1 — Delayed Order`
     - `State 2 — Delivered Not Received`
     - `State 3 — Tracking Not Available Yet`

4. **(Optional) Add Prototype Interactions**:
   - In Figma, switch to the **Prototype** tab on the right panel.
   - Select buttons (e.g. "Support", "Inspect", "Alert Me") and link them between frames with an "On click" -> "Navigate to" transition.

5. **Generate Shareable Link**:
   - Click the blue **Share** button in the top-right corner of Figma.
   - Under link settings, ensure **"Anyone with the link can view"** is selected.
   - Click **Copy link**.
   - Paste the copied URL into your assessment submission field!
